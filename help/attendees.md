# Attendee guide

ProgramLoom's public event pages do not require an account.

## Find a session

Use the search box for a title, topic, speaker, or organization. Filters narrow the program by track or other event choices. The result count changes with your search and filters.

Open a session to read its full description, date, time, room, format, track, and speaker details. Use the visible **Close** or **Back** control to return.

## Browse speakers

The speaker directory is alphabetical by surname. Open a profile for the speaker's biography, job title, organization, headshot, and linked sessions.

## Move between event days

Use the date controls in the agenda or itinerary. The selected date and displayed sessions change together.

## Build your itinerary

Choose **Add to itinerary** on a session you want to attend. Choose **Remove from itinerary** if you change your mind. Your selections stay in the same browser when you reload the page.

Use **Show my schedule only** to focus on your choices. Choose **Export my ICS** to download a calendar file containing the selected sessions.

Your personal itinerary is stored in your browser. Clearing the browser's site data or using another device starts a separate itinerary.
