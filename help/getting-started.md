# Create your first event

This guide gets a new organizing team to a working call for proposals. You do not need to understand every ProgramLoom feature before you begin.

## Before you start

Decide three things:

- the public name of your event;
- the event dates and timezone; and
- the kinds of sessions you want, such as talks, workshops, panels, or lightning talks.

You can change most details later.

## 1. Create the event

From **Events**, choose **Create event**. A starter template gives you a useful structure for a conference, meetup, workshop program, or community call for proposals.

Before ProgramLoom creates the event, it shows what the template will copy. Templates copy reusable setup—not speakers, private notes, old messages, or previous submissions.

## 2. Check the basics

Open the new event and confirm:

- name and public web address;
- dates and timezone;
- tracks or themes;
- session formats and lengths; and
- rooms or locations, if you already know them.

## 3. Prepare the call for proposals

The call for proposals is the public form people use to send session ideas. ProgramLoom often shortens this to **CFP**.

Add the questions you need, mark the essential ones as required, and use conditional questions only when a follow-up applies to a particular answer. Preview the form before publishing it.

## 4. Publish and test it

Choose **Publish CFP**, then open the public link in a private or signed-out browser window. Submit one internal test proposal and confirm it appears in **Submissions**.

## 5. Return to the Control Room

Open **Control Room**. It will show the next real work, such as proposals that need reviewers, decisions waiting to be sent, or accepted speakers who have not entered the portal.
