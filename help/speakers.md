# Speaker guide

The speaker portal keeps your event work in one place.

## Enter the portal

Open the secure link from the event organizer. The event name and your identity appear at the top of the portal. You will only see proposals, sessions, tasks, and files connected to you.

## Update your profile

Open **Profile** to check your preferred name, biography, job title, organization, and headshot. The organizer may publish these details in the event's speaker directory.

Private logistics are visible only to authorized event organizers.

## Complete tasks

Open **Tasks** to see what the event team needs and when it is due. Read the instructions, follow any provided link, then choose **Mark complete** when the work is finished.

## Upload files

Open the matching request before uploading a headshot, slides, handout, or other file. Check the allowed file types and size. Replacing a file creates a new version, so the organizer can understand what changed.

## Respond to feedback

If a file or session needs changes, open its details to read the organizer's comment. Upload a revised version or update the requested wording, then tell the organizer what you changed.

## Check the schedule

After your session is scheduled, the portal shows its date, time, and room. Calendar invitations may arrive by email. If the event team changes important schedule details, the existing calendar event should update.
