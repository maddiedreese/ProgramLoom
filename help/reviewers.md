# Reviewer guide

Reviewers see only the event work they are allowed to review.

## Open your assignments

Use the secure invitation from the organizer. ProgramLoom opens **Your review queue**, where assignments are grouped by review round and completion state.

## Read the proposal

Open an assignment and read the information available for that round. In a blind round, speaker names and other identifying details are hidden.

## Handle a conflict

If you know the submitter, worked closely on the proposal, or cannot evaluate it fairly, use **Declare conflict** or **Recuse**. Add a short explanation when requested. Do not complete the scorecard.

## Complete the review

Answer every required scorecard question, add useful comments, and choose **Complete review**. ProgramLoom confirms the saved state and returns you to the queue.

Your scores and comments are not visible to other reviewers unless the event's process explicitly allows that sharing.

## If something is missing

Contact the organizer if the proposal lacks information, the deadline is wrong, or an assignment should not be yours. Reviewers cannot change event configuration or assign work to themselves.
