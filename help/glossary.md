# Words used in ProgramLoom

- **Agenda** — The event's schedule of sessions, rooms, and times.
- **Call for proposals (CFP)** — The public form people use to suggest a talk, workshop, panel, or other session.
- **Communication** — A message prepared or sent through ProgramLoom, such as a confirmation, decision, reminder, or calendar invitation.
- **Control Room** — The organizer's readiness dashboard. It lists work that is blocking or warning about the program.
- **Decision** — The outcome for a proposal: accepted, waitlisted, or rejected. A staged decision has not been emailed yet.
- **Placement** — The date, start time, end time, and room assigned to a session.
- **Proposal or submission** — An idea sent through the call for proposals. ProgramLoom uses both words for the same record.
- **Review round** — A stage of evaluation with its own reviewers, dates, and scorecard.
- **Reviewer pool** — A group of people who are eligible to receive proposals for a review round.
- **Routing rule** — An organizer's instruction for deciding which reviewer group should receive a proposal.
- **Session** — The program item created from an accepted proposal. A session connects its title and description to speakers, content approval, and agenda placement.
- **Speaker portal** — The private workspace where a speaker updates their profile, completes tasks, uploads files, and reads feedback.
- **Stage decision** — Record the intended outcome without sending a message.
- **Public view or widget** — An attendee-facing schedule, session list, speaker list, speaker gallery, or personal itinerary that can be opened directly or embedded on another website.
