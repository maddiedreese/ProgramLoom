# Prepare speakers

The **Speakers** workspace shows whether each person can enter the portal and whether their onboarding work is complete.

## Invite a speaker

For a speaker without access, choose **Invite speaker** or **Send portal invitation**. The invitation gives that person a private route to their event workspace.

## Collect a complete profile

Ask speakers for the information attendees need, such as:

- preferred name;
- biography;
- job title and organization;
- headshot;
- pronouns or social links, when relevant; and
- private travel or accessibility details for the organizing team.

Public speaker views use only the fields intended for publication. Private logistics stay in authorized organizer views.

## Assign onboarding tasks

Create clear tasks with a description and due date. Examples include confirming the speaker agreement, reviewing the session title, or completing travel details.

The speaker marks a task complete in the portal. Organizer progress updates from that saved task state.

## Work with the speaker database

The organization-wide speaker database helps teams reuse a contact without copying the person. Add the contact to an event to create an event-specific connection, then manage event status, tasks, notes, and communications from the event.
