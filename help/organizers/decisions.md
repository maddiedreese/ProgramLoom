# Make and send decisions

ProgramLoom deliberately separates choosing an outcome from telling the submitter.

## 1. Stage the decision

Open the proposal and choose **Stage decision**. Select accepted, waitlisted, or rejected.

The proposal now shows the intended outcome. **No email is sent.** This lets the organizing team check capacity, program balance, and wording before communication begins.

## 2. Open Communications

Choose **Preview recipients and send decision**, or open the event's **Communications** workspace. Select the decision message and recipient group.

## 3. Preview the real message

Choose a real recipient in the preview. Check that the subject, speaker name, session title, dates, and portal links are correct. ProgramLoom warns about unsupported or unresolved placeholders.

## 4. Send the decision

Choose **Send decision** only after the preview is correct. The outbox records the message as it moves through preparation, the delivery queue, and the email provider.

Statuses have precise meanings:

- **Prepared** — saved but not yet placed in the queue.
- **Queued** — waiting for background delivery.
- **Processing** — currently being handled.
- **Sent** — accepted by the email provider.
- **Delivered** — the provider reported delivery.
- **Bounced** — the receiving address rejected the message.
- **Failed** — delivery could not be completed.
- **Cancelled** — intentionally stopped before completion.

## What acceptance creates

After an acceptance is communicated, ProgramLoom connects the proposal to the speaker, session, portal access, onboarding tasks, communication history, notifications, and audit history needed for the next stage.
