# Calendar invitations and updates

A **calendar invitation** is sent to session participants. It is separate from the public itinerary ICS that an attendee exports from a public widget.

## Send an invitation

After a session has an agenda placement, open the scheduled-session details and choose **Send calendar invitation**. ProgramLoom records the communication and its delivery state.

## Update a scheduled session

Changing the room or time updates the same calendar identity: the UID stays stable and the sequence increases. Review the recipient preview before sending an update.

## Cancel or explicitly reschedule

**Cancel session** produces a calendar cancellation and removes the session from public surfaces. A later reschedule is explicit, retains the stable identity, and advances the sequence safely.
