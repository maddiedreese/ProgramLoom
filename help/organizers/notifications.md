# Notifications

Notifications record durable changes that need attention, including review conflicts, staged decisions, speaker updates, content changes, scheduling conflicts, publication, and delivery failures.

## Find and filter notifications

Open the bell in the top bar. Filter by event, category, severity, or read state. Mark one notification or all visible notifications as read; that state follows your account across browsers.

## Choose delivery preferences

Notification preferences control in-app categories and optional email delivery. Turning off an email preference does not erase the in-app record.

## Recover from a failure

A failure notification links directly to the owning record. Use **Retry delivery** for a retryable message or **Recover integration** for a recoverable integration, then confirm the new durable state.
