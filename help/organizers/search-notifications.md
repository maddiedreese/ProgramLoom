# Search and Command+K

ProgramLoom gives organizers two ways to find work quickly: the command palette and the notification center.

## Search from anywhere

Choose **Search** in the top bar or press **Command+K** on macOS and **Control+K** on Windows or Linux.

Search can find events, proposals, sessions, speakers, contacts, reviewers, tasks, files, resources, saved views, and communication records. Results are grouped by type and include enough event context to distinguish similar names.

You only see records your role is allowed to open. Blind-review details and private fields remain hidden when your role should not see them.

The palette also offers shortcuts such as creating an event, opening the CFP builder, inviting a reviewer, adding a speaker, opening scheduling conflicts, and checking integrations. An action that sends, publishes, or changes important data always opens its normal review or confirmation step.

## Use the notification center

Choose the bell in the top bar to see durable updates such as:

- a new or updated proposal;
- a finished review or reviewer conflict;
- a decision waiting to be sent;
- a speaker, task, file, or content change;
- an agenda conflict or publication;
- a failed delivery, background job, or integration.

Mark one notification or all notifications as read. Filters narrow by event, category, severity, or read state. Read state follows your account across browsers.

## Change notification preferences

Open notification preferences to choose which categories should appear in the app and which may also send email. Turning on email is separate from receiving an in-app notification.
