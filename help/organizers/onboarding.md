# Onboarding tasks

Onboarding tasks prepare an accepted speaker for the event. They are separate from content requests, which collect headshots, decks, and session material.

## Create and track a task

Open **Speakers**, choose a speaker, and add a task with a clear instruction and due date. The speaker sees it in the private speaker portal. Completed and incomplete states are persisted, appear in the Control Room, and remain visible to authorized organizers.

## Complete a task

In the speaker portal, choose **Complete task**. The result panel confirms what changed, the durable completed state, and the next recommended action. An organizer can reopen a task when more work is genuinely required.
