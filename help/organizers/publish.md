# Publish for attendees

Choose **Publish agenda** when session content is approved, required sessions are placed, and conflicts are resolved.

## The five public views

1. **Sessions** — searchable session cards with descriptions, dates, times, rooms, formats, tracks, and speakers.
2. **Speakers** — an alphabetical directory with headshots, biographies, roles, organizations, and linked sessions.
3. **Agenda** — a multi-day schedule with complete session details.
4. **Speaker gallery** — a visual version of the speaker directory.
5. **Personal itinerary** — a schedule where attendees add or remove sessions and export their choices to a calendar file.

These views are public. Attendees do not need a ProgramLoom account.

## Share or embed a view

Open **Widgets** and choose the public view you want. Configure its visible fields, colors, filters, and search options. ProgramLoom gives you a direct link and an embed snippet for another website.

Changing a saved widget configuration updates the existing public view. You do not need to regenerate or republish the embed.

## Keep public information accurate

Public views use the same approved speaker, session, and agenda records organizers see. If you change a published time, room, title, or speaker, check the calendar consequences and republish the agenda when ProgramLoom asks you to.
