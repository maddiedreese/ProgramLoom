# Invite your team

ProgramLoom keeps each organization and event separate and checks access on every saved action.

## Organizer roles

- **Owner** manages the organization, events, integrations, and team access.
- **Admin** can run the full program and manage event operations.
- **Member** can work in permitted organizer areas without owner-only control.
- **Reviewer** sees assigned proposals and scorecards, with blind-review rules applied.
- **Speaker** sees only their own sessions, profile, tasks, files, resources, and messages.

Open organizer registration can create a new organization. Reviewer and speaker access begins with an invitation tied to the intended event and role.

## Invite a reviewer or speaker

Use the visible **Invite reviewer** or **Invite speaker** action in the owning workspace. Preview the recipient, then send the invitation. The invitation and its delivery state remain visible in Communications.

If a person says their link does not work, confirm that they used the invited address and check the invitation's delivery record before retrying.

## Protect private review information

Blind-review settings hide submitter and speaker identity from reviewers where required. A recusal or conflict removes access to the affected review work and alerts the organizer.
