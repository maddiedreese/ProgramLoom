# Use the Control Room

The Control Room is the organizer's home base. It answers one question: **What is keeping this program from being ready?**

## What you will see

ProgramLoom groups work by importance:

- **Blocking** items stop a part of the program from being ready, such as an accepted session with no agenda placement.
- **Warnings** need attention soon but may not block publication, such as an onboarding task approaching its due date.
- **Information** helps the team understand current activity.

Counts come from the event's saved records. Select a count or item to open the exact proposal, speaker, file, message, or schedule view behind it.

## A useful daily routine

1. Open the event's **Control Room**.
2. Start with the first blocking item.
3. Follow its **Resolve**, **Review**, or **Open** action.
4. Finish the underlying work.
5. Return to the Control Room or choose **Refresh**.
6. Continue until the remaining items are understood and assigned.

## Filter the work

For a large event, filter by track, owner, status, deadline, or severity. This helps a program chair, speaker manager, or content lead focus on their part of the event without creating a separate spreadsheet.

## Why an item may remain

Some work has more than one requirement. For example, an accepted session may still appear because its speaker has not entered the portal, its content is not approved, or it has not been scheduled. Open the item to see the specific reason.

If the count looks stale, choose **Refresh**. If it still does not match the underlying records, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#the-control-room-count-does-not-look-right).
