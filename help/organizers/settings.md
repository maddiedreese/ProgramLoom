# Account and organization settings

Personal settings belong to your account. Organization settings control membership and organization-wide connections. Event settings apply only to the selected event.

## Manage access

Owners manage organization membership and sensitive integrations. Organizers only see events in their organization, reviewers only see assigned reviews, and speakers only see their connected speaker records.

## Keep account details safe

Use controlled organization addresses for demonstrations. Do not expose personal addresses on public speaker, session, agenda, widget, help, or marketing surfaces. Revoke tokens and remove access promptly when it is no longer needed.
