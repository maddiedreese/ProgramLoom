# Assign and collect reviews

A review evaluates a proposal. Review rounds help your team perform that work consistently.

## Set up a review round

Open **Reviews** and create a round. Give it a name people will understand, such as “First review” or “Final program review.” Choose:

- when the round opens and closes;
- whether reviewers should see speaker identities;
- which reviewers belong to the pool;
- how many assignments each reviewer can accept; and
- the questions or scores reviewers must complete.

## Assign proposals

Use **Assign reviewers** for a direct assignment, or use **Automatic reviewer routing** for repeatable rules. Before confirming, check the selected proposals and reviewers.

ProgramLoom protects the assignment from known conflicts, recusals, self-review, exclusions, and reviewer capacity.

## Monitor progress

The review workspace shows how many assigned reviews are complete in each round. Filter to find incomplete work, then use **Send reviewer reminder** for the people who still have assignments.

## Read the results

Organizers can compare completed scorecards, comments, recommendations, and overall progress. Blind-review rules apply to reviewers, not authorized organizers.

Choose **Export results** to download a spreadsheet-safe CSV. It contains the proposal aggregate and a separate row for each assigned reviewer, including that review's weighted score, recommendation, comment, and submission time.

Use review evidence to make a decision, but keep the decision and its communication separate. This gives your team a chance to check the full program before anyone receives an email.
